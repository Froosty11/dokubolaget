# UX review, 2026-10-02

## Method

- Three code reviews, one per area: first run and accounts; the game loop; scores, themes and stamps.
- Screenshots of every screen at iPhone Safari's visible height (402×690).
- A native Android run in the emulator.

Every finding was checked against the code. File references are in the three source reviews; the most useful ones are repeated here.

Severity:
- **B** (blocker): the stores will reject the app, or it's broken for everyone.
- **M** (major): many players will notice it.
- **m** (minor).
- **p** (polish).

## Already fixed (checked)

- **Search:** a wrong guess no longer closes search, and search keeps the query.
- **One product per board:** a product used in another cell is rejected.
- **Range tags:** both ends of a range now count, so 13% fits both 10–13% and 13–22%.
- **Screen readers:** board cells are labelled buttons.
- **Native Android:** dialogs crashed, words were cut off after a theme switch, and board headers went blank. All three were fixed on 2026-10-02.

## What works well

- **Near misses:** the message names the header that matched, and that header pulses.
- **Search:** facts the cell asks about are blacked out in search and in the info sheet.
- **Accounts:** account deletion works both in the app and on the web. Password reset doesn't reveal whether an account exists.
- **Players who aren't logged in:** they can play offline, and progress is saved on the device.
- **Responsible drinking:** the helpline note is on Home and in the age gate.
